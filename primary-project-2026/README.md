# primary-project-2026

Data for the story "[We tracked every candidate in the 2026 primaries. Here's who voters are nominating.](https://blog.fiftyplusone.news/p/primary-project-2026)" published on July 31, 2026.

Data from before 2026 was archived from public data released by FiveThirtyEight.

Data from 2026 was gathered by Cooper Burton, Meredith Conroy, and Tia Yang for FiftyPlusOne.news, and will be added to this repository when the primary season is finished. For access to current 2026 data, please reach out to data@fiftyplusone.news.
